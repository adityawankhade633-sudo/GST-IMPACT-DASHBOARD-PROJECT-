Insights & Recommendations

Section 05
🏛 For Policymakers
  • Simplify compliance further for MSMEs through automated return filing systems
  • Rationalize rate structure to reduce classification disputes across sectors
  • Enhance dispute resolution mechanism for faster adjudication timelines
🏢 For Businesses
  • Leverage technology platforms for GST compliance and input credit optimization
  • Restructure supply chains to fully maximize post-GST efficiency benefits
  • Invest in tax planning strategies to optimize working capital flows
🔬 Future Research
  • Longitudinal study on informal sector formalization under GST regime
  • Cross-country comparison with other federal GST systems (Australia, Canada)
  • Environmental impact assessment of logistics optimization post-GST
